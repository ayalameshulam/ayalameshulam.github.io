RoleUX / UI Design

Year2024
WHO

Officers & rabbis

Police officers of every rank and level of observance — and the rabbis who serve them.

WHAT

App + admin system

A mobile app for officers and a web admin system for rabbis and content editors.

WHY

Answers that fit police work

Officers need fast, reliable answers for real situations — Shabbat on duty, kashrut in the field, prayer mid-shift.

Problem statement

Officers lack one accessible, discreet channel for religious services that fits their schedule — and the Rabbinate lacks a structured tool to serve them.

01The challenge

Sensitive questions

Personal topics inside a hierarchical organization → personal details are visible to the rabbi only.

A diverse audience

Secular to Haredi → a warm, neutral tone; content by topic, not by level of observance.

Use on the go

Short breaks, patrol cars, night shifts → large tap targets, 5-tab bottom nav, key info on Home.

A two-sided system

The app is only as fast as the rabbi → a dedicated admin system with statuses and assignment.

03

Project Goals

The app gives officers quick access to trusted religious services and information. It helps the Rabbinate answer questions efficiently and better understand officers’ needs.

Personas

Avi, 32 — Patrol officer

Traditional, rotating shifts including Shabbat.
Goal: a quick answer that fits his shift.
Pain: doesn't know who to ask; uses 3 apps for times and synagogues.

RABBI

Rabbi David, 48 — District rabbi

Answers questions and supports officers.
Goal: see every question in one place and answer fast.
Pain: questions arrive by phone, WhatsApp and SMS, with no tracking.

07

Screens

Home screen

A daily hub: a news ticker, the daily halacha carousel, a featured video and the latest questions and posts.

Ask the Rabbi

Ten topics with icons and search. Every question opens a full Q&A page that can be shared.

Question sent

A clear confirmation, with an option to leave contact details so the rabbi can reply directly.

Videos

A featured lesson and a searchable grid of short videos by Police Rabbinate rabbis.
