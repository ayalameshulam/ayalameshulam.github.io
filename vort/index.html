About VORT

VORT is a technological matchmaking platform that combines the personal, discreet guidance of over 1,000 matchmakers and community representatives. Operating under the guidance of leading rabbis, it manages a high-quality, filtered database that has helped found more than 1,600 homes – while carefully preserving the community’s values.

01

The challenge

The old system had an outdated, inconvenient interface. The challenge was to turn a complex, cluttered system into a clear, organized and easy-to-use one.

Spreadsheet-like screens

Long, dense lists that felt like an Excel sheet, with no room for the eye to rest.

Too many fields, no grouping

Dozens of fields displayed together, without clear groups of information.

A non-intuitive experience

Inconsistent areas and crowded toolbars made the main actions hard to find.

No visual hierarchy

Little use of icons, color or separators – so nothing important stood out.

Problem details

Top bar – many buttons and actions create visual clutter and make it hard to identify the main actions.

Design consistency – inconsistency between different areas of the system creates a non-intuitive user experience.

Tabs area – a large number of tabs without a clear hierarchy, making orientation difficult.

Visual usability – little use of icons, colors and visual separators increases clutter and harms readability.

The form – many fields displayed together without clear grouping of information.

Screen structure – a lot of information on one screen creates clutter and makes quick scanning difficult.

03

Work process

01

Old system

The client sent me all the screens of the old system. I organized them by order and logic – a slow process, since the screens were scattered and the navigation order was unclear.

02

Re-specification

I arranged the old system into an orderly working file, then specified the new system while keeping every important detail from the original.

03

Redesign

The client asked for colors that convey respect and conservatism, so I chose a black-and-gold palette and designed an aesthetic, dignified interface.

Organizing the old system

Every screen of the old system, mapped and grouped by flow before any design work began.

04

User flow

05

UX specification

I mapped the users and analyzed their workflows, defined a new navigation structure, reorganized the information and planned simpler, more efficient flows.

From tables to cards

The old table view was replaced with a modern Card-Based Design. Information appears in clear cards alongside supporting icons – creating a clearer visual hierarchy, better scannability and intuitive, efficient navigation.
